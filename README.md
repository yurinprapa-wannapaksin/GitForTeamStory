## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a beautiful castle on a hill.

   ![castle](img/castle.png)

2. A young princess lived there and dreamed of exploring the world.

3. One day, a mysterious dragon appeared near the castle.

   ![dragon](img/dragon.png)

4. The princess followed the dragon into a forest and met a scientist.

   ![scientist](img/scientist.png)

5. Together, they discovered a treasure chest under a giant tree.

   ![treasure](img/treasure_large.png)
   
6. Inside the chest, they found a map leading to a secret island.

   ![map](img/map.png)

7. They sailed across the ocean but got caught in a terrible storm.

8. On the island, they discovered an ancient temple filled with treasure

   ![treasure_open](img/treasure_open.png)

9. The princess and her friends returned to the castle with the treasure.

10. In the end, they learned that friendship and adventure were more valuable than gold.
